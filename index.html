A small corner of the internet.

Hi, I’m piragi. This is where I write through ideas, share things I’m working on, and keep notes I’ll want to find again.

I’m an ML researcher working on computer vision interpretability — training sparse autoencoders on vision transformers, adapting JLens-style readouts to image models, and building faithful attribution methods. My research has appeared at CVPR 2026 and CHI 2023.

There isn’t a strict theme. The only rule is that each post should leave an idea clearer than I found it.

The site is deliberately small: plain HTML, one stylesheet, and a little JavaScript for themes and mathematical notation. Its look borrows from Eye of the Beholder, the first game I ever played.

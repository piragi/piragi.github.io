Hello, World

24 February 2026

First post. The blog is live. I built it with plain HTML, one CSS file, and a little JavaScript—no framework or build step.

Why a blog

Writing things down forces clarity. A half-formed idea becomes a real one only when you have to put it into words.

The aesthetic is lifted from Eye of the Beholder, the first game I ever played. Dark navy, gold, purple. It felt right.

What this will be

No particular theme. Things I find interesting, things I am working on, and things I want to remember.
